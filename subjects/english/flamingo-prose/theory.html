Flamingo Prose: Kahani Seedhi Bhasha Mein

Paanch chapters: The Last Lesson, Lost Spring, Indigo, Poets and Pancakes, Going Places. Pehle characters pehchano, phir kahani padho.

1. The Last Lesson Alphonse Daudet

Franco-Prussian war (1870-71) ke baad Alsace-Lorraine Prussia ke paas chala gaya. Ab wahan sirf German padhai jayegi. Yehi kahani ki jaan hai.
KaunRole
FranzNarrator, aalsi student
M. HamelFrench teacher, 40 saal se padha rahe hain
Hauser aur gaon ke buzurgAakhri class mein peeche baithe
  • Franz ne participles nahi padhe the, school jaane se darr raha tha.
  • Raaste mein notice-board pe bheed thi, wahan roz buri khabar aati thi.
  • School mein ajeeb khamoshi thi. M. Hamel ne green coat pehna tha aur peeche gaon wale baithe the.
  • Hamel ne bataya: Berlin ka order aaya hai, kal se sirf German. Yeh aakhri French lesson hai.
  • Franz ko apni kitaabon se achanak pyaar ho gaya aur pachtaava hua.
  • Hamel ne apni galti maani aur kaha ki French duniya ki sabse clear, logical bhasha hai. Jab tak log apni bhasha pakde rahte hain, azaadi ki chaabi unke paas hai.
  • 12 baje Hamel bol nahi paaye. Board pe likha "Vive La France!" aur haath se ishara kiya: school khatam.
Yaad rakho: Jo cheez hamesha paas thi, cheenne ke baad hi uski keemat samajh aati hai. Apni bhasha khona matlab apni pehchan khona.

2. Lost Spring Anees Jung

Subtitle: Stories of Stolen Childhood. Do bachhon ki sachi kahani.

KaunRole
SahebBangladesh se aaya ragpicker, Seemapuri (Delhi) mein rehta hai
MukeshFirozabad ka bangle-maker, motor mechanic banna chahta hai
AuthorDono se milti hai

Part 1: Saheb

  • Toofan ne Bangladesh mein unke khet barbaad kiye, isliye parivaar Seemapuri aaya. Wahan hazaaron log bina proper ghar aur pehchan ke rehte hain.
  • Kachre mein bachhe "sona" dhundte hain. Bado ke liye yeh survival hai, bachhon ke liye umeed.
  • Author ne mazaak mein school khulwane ka wada kiya, Saheb baar-baar poochta raha. Wada poora nahi hua.
  • Uska poora naam "Saheb-e-Alam" hai, matlab duniya ka maalik. Irony: wo bhookha aur beghar hai.
  • Aakhir mein chai ki dukaan pe kaam, ₹800 mahina. Par ab wo apna maalik nahi raha, isliye steel canister bhaari lagta hai.

Part 2: Mukesh

  • Firozabad bangles ke liye famous hai. Mukesh ka poora khaandaan generations se yehi kaam karta hai.
  • Bhattiyan garam, andheri aur bina hawa ki hain. Bachhon ki aankhon ki roshni jaldi chali jaati hai.
  • Dadi ke liye yeh "karam" hai, badla nahi ja sakta. Caste aur gareebi ne unhe yehi sikhaya.
  • Budhi aurat: "maine kabhi pet bhar khana nahi khaya".
  • Middlemen, police aur sahukar unhe sangathan banane se rokte hain.
  • Mukesh alag sochta hai: motor mechanic banunga. Par uska sapna bhi sheher ke andar hi seemit hai.
Yaad rakho: "Lost Spring" matlab bachpan ka khoya hua bahaar. Gareebi aur tradition ne dono ka bachpan cheen liya.

3. Indigo Louis Fischer

Gandhi ka pehla satyagraha, Champaran (Bihar), 1917. Louis Fischer ki Gandhi biography se.
KaunRole
GandhiKisano ki madad karne aate hain
Rajkumar ShuklaGarib kisan, Gandhi ko Champaran laata hai
ZamindarKisano se zabardasti indigo ugwate the
British CommissionerGandhi ko Champaran chhodne ka order deta hai
  • Tinkathia system: kisan ko zameen ke 3/20 hisse (15%) pe indigo ugana padta tha aur wo zamindar ko dena padta tha.
  • Shukla ki zid se Gandhi Champaran pahunche aur facts jutane lage.
  • Commissioner ne chhodne ka order diya, Gandhi ne mana kiya. Court mein hazaaron kisan unke saath aa gaye, yeh unke darr se azaad hone ki shuruaat thi.
  • Gandhi ne kaha ki wo kanoon nahi todna chahte, par gareebon ki madad unka farz hai. Case wapas liya gaya. Yeh civil disobedience ki pehli jeet thi.
  • Commission mein zamindar paise wapas karne ko maane. Gandhi ne 50% maanga, par 25% pe maan gaye, kyunki zamindaron ka ghamand tootna zyada zaroori tha.
  • Gandhi ne school, safai aur health camps bhi shuru kiye.
  • Andrews madad karna chahte the, Gandhi ne mana kiya: apne bal pe khade ho (self-reliance).
Yaad rakho: Satya aur ahimsa se badi power ko bhi challenge kiya ja sakta hai. Kisano ka darr khatam hona sabse zaroori tha.

4. Poets and Pancakes Asokamitran

Yeh seedhi kahani nahi hai. Yeh Gemini Studios (Chennai) ke chhote-chhote funny kisse hain, halke satirical andaaz mein. Isliye har character ko alag se samjho.
KaunRole
Author (Asokamitran)Studio mein kaam karta tha
The BossStudio ka maalik
Office boyMake-up department mein, Subbu se jalta tha
Kothamangalam SubbuStudio ka No. 2: poet, writer, actor
Legal AdviserStory Department ka hissa
Stephen SpenderEnglish poet jo studio aaye, studio walon ko pata hi nahi tha wo kaun hain
  • Make-up room mein alag-alag pradesh ke log saath kaam karte the. Author mazaak mein ise "national integration" kehta hai.
  • Subbu bahut talented tha aur Boss ke kareeb tha, isliye kuch log jalte the.
  • Legal Adviser: Story Department band hui, poets ghar bheje gaye, aur usi department ka hissa hone se lawyer ki naukri bhi gayi. Yehi irony hai.
  • Studio mein aate poets ki politics shallow thi. Wo khadi pehente the aur Communism se darte the, bina uske baare mein jaane.
  • Spender ki mystery baad mein solve hui jab author ne unke baare mein padha. Wo pehle Communist the, baad mein anti-Communist ban gaye.
Yaad rakho: Chapter dikhata hai ki studio mein status aur importance kitni khokhli (hollow) thi.

5. Going Places A. R. Barton

Gareeb ghar ki ladki Sophie apni boring zindagi se bhaagne ke liye imagination ka sahara leti hai.
KaunRole
SophieDreamer, boutique ya actress banna chahti hai
JansieSophie ki dost, practical
GeoffBada bhai, motorcycle mechanic, kam bolta hai
PapaThake hue, Sophie ki baaton pe yakeen nahi karte
Danny CaseyFamous young footballer
  • Jansie jaanti hai dono ko factory mein hi kaam karna hoga. Sophie sapne dekhti hai.
  • Geoff Sophie ke liye ek exciting alag duniya ka symbol hai.
  • Sophie Geoff ko kehti hai ki wo Danny Casey se mili. Geoff pehle shak karta hai, phir details sunke maan jaata hai.
  • Papa mazaak udaate hain.
  • Sophie canal ke paas akeli Danny ka intezaar karti hai aur apne mann mein poori mulakat imagine karti hai.
  • Danny nahi aata. Sophie udaas ho jaati hai.
Samajhne wali baat: Kahani saaf nahi bolti, par canal wale scene se lagta hai ki mulakat Sophie ki fantasy thi. Escape ne use aur akela aur udaas banaya.
