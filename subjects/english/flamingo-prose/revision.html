Flamingo Prose: Quick Revision

Exam se pehle bas yeh padh lo. Har chapter ka core, ek jagah.

The Last Lesson: Language = identity = freedom. Berlin order, last French lesson, Hamel's "Vive La France!". Franz ko loss ke baad value samajh aati hai.
Lost Spring: Stolen childhood. Saheb (ragpicker, lost freedom at tea-stall). Mukesh (bangle-maker, dreams of being a mechanic). Poverty, caste, middlemen, no cooperative.
Indigo: Champaran 1917, tinkathia system. Gandhi ka pehla satyagraha. Fear se azaadi. 25% refund, kyunki prestige zyada important tha. Self-reliance.
Poets and Pancakes: Gemini Studios, satire. Subbu No. 2. Legal adviser ki naukri poets ke saath gayi (irony). Status khokhla hai.
Going Places: Sophie vs Jansie. Geoff = alag duniya. Danny Casey se mulakat sirf fantasy. Escape se udaasi badhi.

Linked pairs (SQP pattern)

  • Indigo + Poets and Pancakes: authority. Gandhi resists it through moral courage. Asokamitran exposes its hollowness through humour.
  • Keeping Quiet + Going Places: response to inner conflict. Silence as reflection versus imagination as escape.

Exam tips

  • Long answers: point 1 for one text, point 2 for the other, then a one-line comparison.
  • Likhte waqt chapter ke naam aur author ke naam zaroor likho.
  • Irony wale questions mein "contrast" word use karo.
