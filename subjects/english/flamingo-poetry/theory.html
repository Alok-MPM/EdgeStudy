Keeping Quiet Pablo Neruda

Chile ke Nobel Prize-winning poet. Is poem mein wo duniya, shanti aur insaan ke andar ki baatein dekhte hain.

Ek line mein: Poet kehta hai ki hum kuch der ek saath ekdum shaant ho jaayein. Isse hum khud ko aur duniya ko behtar samjhenge.

Poem seedhi bhasha mein

  • Poet kehta hai: baarah tak gino aur sab ek saath sthir ho jaao. Na bolna, na bhaagna.
  • Ek ajeeb par khoobsurat pal hoga. Koi jaldi nahi, koi shor nahi.
  • Agar sab ruk jaayein, toh samundar mein machhuware whales ko nuksan nahi pahunchayenge, aur namak banane wala apne ghaayal haathon ko dekhega.
  • Jo log yudh ki taiyari karte hain, wo bhi ruk jaayein toh hinsa ruk sakti hai.
  • Poet saaf kehta hai ki wo maut ya total inactivity ki baat nahi kar raha. Wo sirf ek meaningful pause maangta hai.
  • Earth ka example: sardiyon mein sab shaant lagta hai, par zindagi andar chalti rehti hai.
  • Poem ke end mein poet ek baar phir baarah tak ginne ko kehta hai aur khud chala jaata hai.
Yaad rakho: Poem "kuch mat karo" nahi kehti. Yeh kehti hai: ek pal ruk kar khud ko samjho. Yeh khamoshi maut nahi, samajhdaari hai.

Key ideas

  • Theme: self-reflection, peace, harmony with nature
  • Tone: calm, gentle, persuasive
  • Message: restless activity ka thoda rukna zaroori hai
