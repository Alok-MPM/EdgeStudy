Keeping Quiet: Quick Revision

Poet: Pablo Neruda (Chile, Nobel Prize in Literature).
Central idea: Thoda ruk kar khud ko aur duniya ko samjho. Silence = self-awareness, peace.
  • Counting to twelve = ek short, purposeful pause
  • Whales aur salt-gatherer = hum jo nuksan karte hain, uska ehsaas
  • War-makers ruk jaayein toh hinsa ruk sakti hai
  • Poet maut ya inactivity nahi maang raha, sirf ek temporary pause
  • Earth in winter = stillness mein bhi zindagi
